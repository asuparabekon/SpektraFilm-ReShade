Lens effects: diffraction and prescription-based flare

Independent GPL-3.0 shader/baker implementation. No RealBloom or Realflare
source code is copied into this package. Algorithm references:
- RealBloom (bean-mhm): Fourier aperture diffraction and image convolution.
  https://github.com/bean-mhm/realbloom
- Realflare (Beat Reichenbach): two-interface reflection paths, pupil clipping,
  spectral glass response and single-layer antireflection coatings.
  https://github.com/beatreichenbach/realflare

Lens geometry is derived from the existing DeepLens prescriptions shipped in
lens-designs/. Their pinned revision, Apache-2.0 license and patent/design
identifications are recorded in LENS-NOTICES.txt. This does not reproduce a
measured retail lens, and is not a full Realflare ray-tracer port.

Baked lens-effect metadata is retained in ../provenance/.
Output: Spektra-Diffraction.png, Spektra-LensEffects.fxh and
lens-effects-assets.json. The metadata records each input design hash.

Diffraction: 512-square pupil FFT; five through nine blades, three rounded
blade states. Radial tail energy is integrated from the FFT, then reconstructed
as symmetric edge-normal spokes with 8/16 samples per axis on the GPU. RGB
wavelengths scale the footprint. It is a compressed tail approximation, not
full FFT convolution; the direct image and existing geometric blur are kept.
Physical scale is wavelength times f-number in sensor millimetres. Size values
above one are deliberate artistic enlargement. A round prefiltered component
approximates rounded open blades. The 5/7/8/8 blade profile presets and curvature
are assumptions inspired by these lens families, not measured aperture masks.

Ghosts: first-order/paraxial propagation through the lens surfaces, two
reflections per path, clear-aperture clipping, Abbe-derived Cauchy dispersion,
and six selected paths per profile. Aspheres use their vertex curvature.
The GPU approximates each ghost by a pupil-shaped filtered image transform;
complex caustics and true field-dependent distortion are not represented.
Coating design wavelengths of 500/535/570 nm are assumed, not measured. Ghost
energy is normalized for an artistic strength control, not calibrated radiometry.

Aperture linking shares the existing three aperture selections with diffraction
size/blade state and ghost footprint/throughput. Geometric lens softness and
vignetting already use that selection. Manual overrides retain independent
profile, aperture, blade count, rotation, size, intensity and coating colour.
No DOF, aperture-driven exposure change, CPU readback or runtime ray tracing.
All new optical effects use the lens-transformed source and run before film
response. Film damage and grain remain late. Off-screen light is unavailable.
