Changes: ReShade/HLSL port of box downsampling and tent reconstruction;
seven aspect-correct scales, normalized scale blending and threshold-free
scene-light redistribution. No Unity runtime or anti-flicker brightness
weighting is used. Capture and light redistribution use the lens-transformed
image. Diffusion remains a separate, unchanged stage.
