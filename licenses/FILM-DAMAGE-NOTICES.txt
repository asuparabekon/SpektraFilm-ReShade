Original empty 35mm Lomochrome Purple film scans and annotations, used in
Simulating analogue film damage to analyse and improve artefact restoration
on high-resolution scans (Eurographics 2023).

Spektra-FilmDamage.png contains individual defects extracted from scans 1, 2,
3, 5 and 8. Annotation masks select the scanned pixels. Local background
suppression removes scanner grain; grayscale coverage is retained. Scratch
orientation is normalized to the film transport direction. Crops are padded
and resampled into a linear coverage atlas: red=dust, green=hair, blue=scratch.

64 dust examples, 64 hair examples and 39 distinct scratches populate 64
slots per channel. Scratch slots repeat after exhausting the unique examples.
SHA-256 source hashes and annotation identifiers are in film-damage-assets.json.
Extraction metadata is retained in ../provenance/film-damage-assets.json.
