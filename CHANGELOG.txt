Initial standalone ReShade publication

- Adapted spektrafilm's exposure, development and print response to staged GPU LUTs.
- Packed 33-cubed exposure/print tables into two 8-bit planes for 16-bit values,
  with 2048-sample film characteristic curves and 20 film / 8 print profiles.
- Added softer paper variants and neutral-pivot density compression for the
  artistic paper-contrast control; original upstream profile notices are unchanged.
- Adapted vkdt's density-domain grain model to ReShade's film/print pipeline.
- Added pre-grading, automatic exposure, optical diffusion, halation, lens profiles,
  veiling glare, diffraction, flares, lens dirt and scanned film defects.
- Added shared compiler-compatible generation for Windows and Proton.
- Published the standalone shader and source modules without game-specific addons.
